# Hollow Choir — music requests for Adrian

Check existing tracks first. These are creative directions, not claims about any particular Suno
version, paid feature, generated duration, loop precision or stem quality. Paste the prompt text into
your normal music-generation workflow. Instrumental means no sung words; wordless ensemble is welcome
where specified. Send the original download plus any title/prompt/link you have, or supply its local path.
Use the [audio contract](../../assets/audio/AUDIO_CONTRACT.md) and intake template for delivery.

Generate one or two candidates per needed cue rather than a large batch. First establish one approved
motif; if you later use reference audio for other tracks, record the actual reference and audition the
relationship. Independent text prompts cannot guarantee the same melody or synchronized stems.

## briarfen-battle

**Priority 1 · Measured Violence — Briarfen · target 110 BPM, 4/4, 90–150 seconds.**
Use an existing suitable battle track before generating this. It should survive many normal fights.

```text
Original instrumental combat music for Hollow Choir, a dark pixel-art RPG in a flooded fungal marsh.
Urgent, tactical and earthy, with curiosity beneath the danger. Steady 110 BPM in 4/4. Tight soft-edged
frame drums, low toms, dry plucked strings, cello ostinato, restrained electronic pulse, irregular wooden
clicks and occasional distant tarnished-bell tones. A short economical melodic idea, with an orderly
ritual phrase gently disturbed by living acoustic textures. Keep the pulse steady, the arrangement
moderate and the mid/high frequencies spacious so game impact and reaction sounds stay audible.
Introduce useful energy immediately, vary instrumentation gently, and return naturally to the opening
for repeat listening. Aim for roughly two minutes. No lyrics, spoken voice, trailer booms, cymbal wash,
EDM drop, victory finale, silence break or sudden tempo change. Not wall-to-wall horror. Original music;
do not imitate a named artist, composer or soundtrack.
```

## briarfen-boss-mirebell

**Priority 2 · The Bell That Would Not Fall Silent · target 126 BPM, 4/4, 120–180 seconds.**
For the already-existing Mirebell Cantor; one mix throughout, with no new boss phases.

```text
Original instrumental ritual boss music for Hollow Choir's Mirebell Cantor: an ancient marsh bell
bound in living roots. Restrained menace, tragic grandeur and stubborn life, not a generic evil choir.
Steady 126 BPM in 4/4. A repeating tuned bronze-bell phrase, low bowed strings, dry frame drums,
wooden knocks, dark plucked strings and a distant wordless vowel ensemble. Start with beautiful ordered
harmony; let asymmetrical organic accents disagree without changing the underlying tempo. More weight
than an ordinary battle, but leave clear space for combat cues. Develop through texture rather than
fake dramatic phase changes. Keep a consistent useful intensity, with an ending that returns naturally
to the first phrase. Aim for two to three minutes. No lyrics, spoken chants, huge trailer percussion,
shrill sustained leads, EDM drop, abrupt silence or mandatory final climax. Original composition;
do not imitate an existing artist or game soundtrack.
```

## global-title

**Priority 3 · A Bell Beneath the Soil · target 78 BPM, 4/4, 120–180 seconds.**
An existing reflective track may already fill this role. Establish the game's motif here if possible.

```text
Original instrumental main-menu theme for Hollow Choir, a dark pixel-art fantasy world caught between
perfect divine preservation and uncontrolled organic life. Melancholic wonder, ancient mystery and
restrained human hope. Approximately 78 BPM, primarily 4/4. Begin with a memorable economical five-to-
seven-note idea on felt piano or delicate plucked strings. Gradually add low cello, glass harmonics,
faint detuned bronze bells, subtle wooden percussion and a distant wordless ensemble that initially
sounds beautiful. Let the clean sustained harmony gently disagree with imperfect acoustic instruments.
Intimate and spacious rather than cinematic bombast. A modest arrival, then return toward the opening
without fully resolving its ambiguity; suitable for extended menu listening. Aim for two to three
minutes. No lyrics, spoken voice, startling impacts, trailer drums or imitation of a named composer.
```

## Return checklist

- Original WAV if available; original M4A, MP3 or FLAC downloads are also accepted. Keep original bytes/name.
- Intended cue, title, creator/provider, any source link, generation date and prompt you know.
- Notes such as “love this section,” “too busy,” or the section you'd like to loop.
- Any available usage evidence; unknown information stays unknown until reviewed.

Do not spend time trimming loops or making stems before we hear the existing tracks. We can choose
roles and preparation work from the actual audio instead of generating replacements blindly.
